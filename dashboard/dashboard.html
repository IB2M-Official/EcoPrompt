About Creator IB2M & EcoPrompt

EcoPrompt was created by IB2M to bring radical, guilt-free environmental transparency to generative AI applications. As millions of queries are sent daily to large language models, the computational and thermodynamic cost remains hidden behind pristine web interfaces.

100% Private & In-Browser: Unlike analytics services that harvest your prompts or track your identity, EcoPrompt operates completely offline in your browser. Calculations are based on localized algorithmic models derived from peer-reviewed research (Luccioni et al., Google Sustainability, Patterson et al.).

Article 2

AI Resource Trends: Past, Present & Future

From 2020 to 2023, standard text generation (GPT-3, early PaLM) consumed roughly 1.5 to 2.5 Wh per request. With the rise of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures and distillation (GPT-4o mini, Gemini Flash), light queries dropped to ~0.3 Wh.

However, the 2024–2026 paradigm introduced Test-Time Compute (Reasoning / Thinking models) such as OpenAI o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1, and Gemini Deep Think. These models iterate over hidden chains of thought, multiplying GPU cluster execution time by 4× to 10× per answer.

Multimodal diffusion models (DALL-E 3, Imagen 3, Sora) represent the heaviest consumer tier, requiring hundreds of matrix passes per image, reaching 30+ Wh per generation.

Article 3

Provider Footprint: OpenAI vs Google vs Anthropic

Not all clouds are powered equally. The location and energy mix of the hosting data center drastically influence the carbon intensity (PUE & CUE):

  • Google (Gemini): Operates on custom TPUv4/TPUv5e silicon with highly optimized 24/7 carbon-free energy goals, achieving some of the lowest PUE metrics in the industry (~1.10).
  • Microsoft / OpenAI (ChatGPT): Relies on massive Azure NVIDIA H100/B200 clusters. Carbon footprint varies widely depending on whether data centers reside in hydro-rich regions or fossil-heavy grids.
  • Anthropic (Claude on AWS/GCP): Utilizes distributed Trainium and NVIDIA clusters with balanced energy efficiency profiles.
Article 4

The 4 Environmental & Financial Pillars

⚡ Energy (Watt-hours)

Electrical draw of server power supplies, GPU tensor cores, high-bandwidth memory (HBM3), and data center auxiliary power (PUE multiplier).

💧 Water Consumption (mL)

Direct on-site evaporative cooling towers + indirect water consumed at thermoelectric power generation plants (WUE factor).

🍃 Carbon Emissions (g CO₂)

Grams of equivalent CO₂ calculated using regional grid emission factors (~300–450 g CO₂/kWh global average data center mix).

💶 Estimated Cost (€)

Approximate equivalent token API value based on standard public enterprise rates for average response lengths.

Article 5

How EcoPrompt Works & Calculates

When you press Enter or click Send on ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, EcoPrompt executes the following pipeline:

  1. DOM Interception: Securely extracts prompt length without saving or transmitting any text content.
  2. Length Weighting: Standard prompts (~400 tokens) use base coefficients. Longer prompts (>1,000 chars ×1.3, >3,000 chars ×1.8) reflect increased KV-cache computation.
  3. Local Storage Update: Aggregates daily, weekly, and monthly counters in chrome.storage.local.
  4. Quota Check: Evaluates your custom threshold and alerts you if budget is surpassed.
